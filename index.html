NetShield Knowledge Base

IP & Network Intelligence

An IP address alone does not prove whether a connection is trustworthy. NetShield combines IP intelligence, organization, ASN, timezone and browser-side signals to produce a contextual diagnostic result.

Browser Fingerprinting

Canvas, WebGL, screen metrics, language, touch support, hardware concurrency and automation signals can contribute to a browser fingerprint. They are diagnostic signals, not a guaranteed physical identity.

Offers & Traffic Diagnostics

NetShield can help identify consistency problems around IP, timezone, browser and device signals. It does not guarantee approval by an offer network and does not replace that network's own fraud or policy systems.
