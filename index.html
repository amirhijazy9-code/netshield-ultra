💡 Privacy & Offer-Platform Tips

Consistent timezone VPN/proxy users can encounter mismatches when the browser timezone does not correspond to the apparent network region.
Browser consistency Unusual combinations of browser, OS, language, screen and platform signals may increase automated risk scoring.
IP reputation Shared or previously abused IP addresses can have poor reputation. This is something an IP-intelligence database can evaluate.
Privacy tools Privacy extensions can change browser behavior. More privacy does not automatically mean every website will accept the resulting fingerprint.
Why an offer may not count Tracking systems can fail because of blocked cookies, network errors, attribution windows, duplicate conversions, invalid traffic rules or advertiser-side rejection.
Don't bypass platform rules If a service rejects traffic, investigate the legitimate reason instead of attempting to evade its fraud or abuse controls.
Secure connection HTTPS protects traffic in transit, but it does not hide your IP from the websites you visit.
Fingerprinting A browser fingerprint is composed from multiple signals. No single signal proves that a user is fraudulent.
